# PPO signal diagnosis after the fresh 40-seed suite

No model, reward, feature implementation or training job was changed during
this diagnosis. New scripts read saved data and run frozen-policy diagnostics.

## Measured findings

1. **The evaluation mean is tail-sensitive.** Baseline's three highest scores
   are 821, 634 and 381. Excluding those same three seed pairs changes final
   run-1 mean delta from -35.275 to +2.108, and run-2 from -31.2 to +8.135.
   This is sensitivity analysis, not grounds to discard the seeds: high scores
   matter to the expected-score objective. Their selection also creates a
   winner's-curse risk when interpreting one sampled trajectory as capability.

2. **No single new failure mode explains these lost runs.** Final run 1 dies
   to an adder on D:2, a gnoll on D:5, and liquid fire on D:3 for these seeds.
   Final run 2 dies to an ogre on D:4, constriction on D:2, and a scorpion on
   D:4. Several characters have removed their body armour, but so did one of
   the high-scoring baseline characters. This does not isolate armour removal
   as the causal difference. Firewood attacks actually decreased in aggregate.

3. **Training has little long-game outcome coverage.** Counts across both
   runs: D:1 11,116; D:2 4,175; D:3 913; D:4 180; deeper floors zero. There
   are only 17 terminal segments. Most segments stop at a collection boundary
   and rely on the critic. The actor lanes continue many games, but rotating
   lanes and the four-cycle budget still leave the collection heavily early-D.

4. **The critic loses navigation information.** Visit-memory and route
   descriptors enter the policy through action features. `state_value()`
   replaces the entire action-feature vector with zeros. A controlled probe
   changes visitation counts and stagnation counters: the policy's action
   tensor changes, while the critic's state tensor is bit-identical. The
   critic still sees local geometry/history and coarse outside-map counts;
   it is not entirely blind to exploration, but lacks the policy's explicit
   navigation-memory descriptors.

5. **The critic is not yet a trustworthy long-horizon guide.** On the
   death-ending segments, explained variance of collected V predictions vs
   actual remaining score is negative in five of six post-first-cycle checks
   (-.130, -.396, +.012, -.095, -.056, -.220). This audit is conditioned on
   death and is NOT an unbiased general calibration study. Nevertheless it
   offers no evidence that the critic already supplies reliable long-horizon
   credit. Value-derived terms contribute appreciably to GAE advantages.

6. **Short-term credit is plausible but not proven as the cause.** With
   gamma 1 and lambda .95, direct reward influence at 100 decisions is
   .95^100 = .00592. This is not a hard horizon: a good critic could propagate
   later outcomes. Here 37 of 38 downstairs decisions receive positive
   batch-normalized advantages. All immediate native score deltas for those
   actions are zero; their combined immediate exploration reward is 323.314
   score units. This suggests testing premature-descent credit, not asserting
   that exploration credit alone caused the regression or adding a stair ban.

7. **Exploration is limited but not absent.** Depending on cycle, 35–50% of
   training states put >99% probability on one action. Across the saved
   batches, only 20 ability actions occur. More conservative updates alone
   cannot create examples of alternatives that rarely get sampled.

## Proposed next implementation, not yet performed

Keep the actor frozen initially. Collect longer/complete games from training
seeds, preserve their actual score-to-go, and first demonstrate useful critic
prediction on separate training-development seeds. Give the state-value model
access to navigation-memory summaries and/or a pooled representation of legal
actions, without treating zeros as equivalent to the state information in
those actions. Then compare bounded policy updates using the improved critic
against a longer-return/Monte-Carlo control. Keep reward and actor architecture
fixed initially so the test can isolate credit assignment. A poor critic should
be identified before its gradients guide another broad actor-training batch.

The resampling diagnostic must also inform interpretation: if the baseline's
original large scores rarely recur, do not train bespoke fixes to those exact
trajectories and call that restored general capability.

## Subsequent completion notes (2026-09-14 history audit)

The 36-game resampling diagnostic completed. Across the selected three seeds
and four new action-sampling continuations each, mean native scores were
baseline 141.75, run-1 final 156.833, run-2 final 148.0. The original baseline
scores 821/634/381 were not stable across resampling. These are outcome-selected
seeds, so this comparison cannot reverse the fresh-suite conclusion or justify
promotion. See [comparison](artifacts/ppo-resampling-diagnosis-01/comparison.json).

The frozen-actor critic collection/ablation proposed above was subsequently
implemented and completed on 48 seeds. Its [results](CRITIC-EXPERIMENT.md) show
only a small, tail-sensitive prediction difference on seven usable test games;
no actor update or improved-critic PPO comparison has been established. Thus
the collection/critic part of the old proposal is no longer pending, while
the policy-improvement claim remains unproven. The latest unexecuted proposal
and related earlier attempts are distinguished in the
[ledger](../EXPERIMENT-LEDGER.md).
