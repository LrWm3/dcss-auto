# Simulator artifact cleanup — 2026-09-14

Completed at the user's request. Scope was only this repository's tactical and
autonomous simulator artifact directories. The old live WebTiles game, source
checkout, other projects and unrelated workspace data were left alone.

## Space recovered

| Measurement | Before | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Entire workspace, `du -h` | ~333 GiB | ~53 GiB |
| Filesystem available, `df -h` | ~22 GiB | ~302 GiB |

Approximately **280 GiB of allocated disk space recovered**. The logical byte
totals below are smaller because millions of small cache files also consumed
filesystem allocation overhead. Numbers above are rounded tool measurements.

1. Removed **9,057,341 regenerable private engine-cache files**, in 34,042
   `saves/db` and `saves/des` directories: **172.665 GiB logical bytes**.
   Shared cache templates, character saves and engine binaries were retained.
   Unknown contents, symlinks, recently modified caches and failure directories
   were excluded. No artifact-using live simulator was detected.
2. Losslessly compressed **10,361 old tactical worker traces** (`transitions.jsonl`
   and `events.jsonl`): **103.460 GiB → 9.890 GiB**, saving **93.570 GiB**.
   Every gzip was fully read back and SHA256-verified before the exact original
   was removed. Canonical datasets and general-policy replay were excluded.

the reduction. The remaining ~53 GiB is local research material and tooling,

archives themselves have individual SHA256 content verification.

## Recovery and repeat maintenance

Cache bytes were deleted, not backed up. They are regenerable using the
associated engine and retained templates. Do not interpret cache deletion as
permission to discard the engines or scenario saves.

Compressed traces are recoverable exactly. Some historical tactical scripts
still expect the raw `.jsonl` filename; restore only the needed file first:

```sh
gzip -dk /absolute/path/to/worker-example/transitions.jsonl.gz
```

This retains the gzip and consumes additional space for the restored raw trace.
No broad restore is necessary for current general-policy training.
